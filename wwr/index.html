More Than a Rafting Trip

Guided Adventures for Every Skill Level

Choose from shorter introductory outings and full-day river challenges. Each trip begins with a safety briefing and is led by guides who understand changing river conditions.

Equipment Prepared for the River

Essential equipment such as personal flotation devices, helmets, paddles, and dry bags is prepared before departure. Select trips also include wetsuits or additional expedition equipment.
