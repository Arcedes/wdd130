Featured Rafting Trips

Half Day Adventure

A welcoming introduction to white water rafting with manageable rapids, scenic river sections, and time to practice teamwork with your guide.

  • Duration: approximately 4 hours
  • Difficulty: beginner to intermediate
  • Equipment: raft, paddle, helmet, PFD, and dry bag

Big Mallard Full Day

Spend a complete day reading rapids, paddling through technical sections, and enjoying a riverside break with a trained guide.

  • Duration: approximately 7 hours
  • Difficulty: intermediate
  • Equipment: raft, paddle, helmet, PFD, dry bag, and lunch gear

Desolation Canyon Expedition

An extended river journey combining larger rapids, canyon scenery, camp preparation, and multiple days away from the road.

  • Duration: 3 days and 2 nights
  • Difficulty: intermediate to advanced
  • Equipment: expedition raft, safety gear, dry bags, and camp kit
